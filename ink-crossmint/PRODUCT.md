# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ink CrossMint serves crypto developers, technical evaluators, early adopters, and investors who need to understand how Ink SDK can simplify multi-chain product experiences. Users arrive expecting a working demo: connect a wallet, mint on Sui, generate a Monad proof, and inspect how the unified signing flow is structured.

## Product Purpose

Ink CrossMint is a developer demo showing how Ink SDK can power cross-platform crypto experiences. Users mint an NFT on Sui, generate a Monad proof, and interact through one unified signing flow. Success means the product feels credible as a public MVP, not a mock landing page, and makes the distinction between “minted on Sui” and “linked to Monad” precise.

## Brand Personality

Secure, premium, technical. The interface should feel like a serious signing and identity layer for builders: calm enough to trust, sharp enough to demo, and clear enough for developers to audit the architecture.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a childish NFT drop, speculative DeFi casino, generic purple Web3 landing page, or fake “omnichain” bridge claim. Avoid implying the NFT moves to Monad unless bridging is actually implemented.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what Ink promises: one clean flow across multiple signing contexts.
2. Make chain boundaries visible without making them feel fragmented.
3. Lead with working product behavior before marketing explanation.
4. Prefer precise technical claims over hype language.
5. Keep trust cues close to the action: addresses, networks, proof hashes, transaction states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-focusable controls, readable errors, and reduced-motion fallbacks. The interface should not depend on color alone for transaction/proof states.
